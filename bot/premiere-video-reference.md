## 4. Editing Tools

**Length:** 7:38 · **Link:** https://youtu.be/PCW-W_BYDSI · **Video ID:** `PCW-W_BYDSI`

The core toolbar tools and timeline navigation.

**Key points**

- Zoom the timeline with the scroll bar handles or the **+ and − keys**. Separate bars adjust video and audio track height.
- **V — Select tool.** Moves clips, trims by dragging clip edges. V always returns you here.
- **Cmd+Z** undo. Use it constantly.
- **A — Track Select Forward.** Selects everything to the right of the cursor. Prevents accidentally overwriting clips you can't see off-screen.
- **C — Razor.** For precise cuts, park the playhead where you want the cut, then hover the razor over the playhead and click.
- Tracks can be muted; a muted video or audio track exports silent or blank.
- **Pen tool** — draws masks in the program monitor, but mostly used to add points to the audio level line for fades and dips.
